## v0.1.0 — 2026-10-06

### New — First Public Release
- One window for all your characters: every character down the left, one character's checklist on the right. A character appears after you log into it once with Mnemosyne running
- Seven categories read from the game (story, jobs, events, dailies and weeklies, content, collections, money) and My tasks, the tasks you add yourself
- All tasks: everything still to do on any character in one list, with pin, priority, snooze until the next reset, and hide
- All characters: a sortable table of every character, with gil, ventures ready, bag space and company seals
- Totals: each currency added up across your characters, and a graph of your gil over time
- Roles: give a character a role, such as a retainer mule, and only what the role tracks counts toward its number
- One chat line at login and a count in the server info bar; optional reminders for the daily reset, events ending, Wondrous Tails, house visits and My tasks before reset
- Export and import, to move your characters to another PC or keep a copy
- An optional button that switches to a character through Lifestream
- Nothing leaves your PC: Mnemosyne has no network code, does not use the Lodestone, and never writes to the game
